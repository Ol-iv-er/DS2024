### What is Statistics?
**Statistics**: is the science of collecting, organizing, and analyzing data. -> Results in better decision makaing <br>
**Data**: Facts or pieces of information that can be measured.
-Examples:
1. The IQ of a class of students. {99, 102, 88, 100, 110, 101, 87, 88}
2. Age of students in a class. {22, 21, 23, 27, 20}

## Types of Statistics 
1. Descriptive Statistics: Consist of organizing and summarzing data

2. Inferential Statistics: Technique where we use the data that we have collected/measured to form conclusions.

Example: Classroom of math students (20 students), Marks: {88, 86, 87, 90, 79, 92, 96, 99, ...}
- For Descriptive stats: We would like to know what is average score of the students in the class, we can also look at the quantiles of the scores.

- For inferential stats: We would like to answer questions like; Are the grades of the students of the class similar to the grades of students in other math classes in the college?

Population: College -> Sample: Class

## Population and Sample

Eg. Election: The population is the united states and we could take samples from difference states and see where canidates stand.

Population is usually denoted with (N) and Sample is with a (n)

## Sampling Techniques

1. Simple Random Sampling (SRS)
    - When performing SRS every member of the population has an equal chance of being chosen.

2. Stratified Sampling
    - Sampling technique where the population (N) is split into non-overlapping groups (strata)
eg.  Gender -> Female and Male
